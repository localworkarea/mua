Bridal trial

Your trial: where the look comes together.

A bridal trial is a dedicated appointment ahead of your wedding day. It’s a chance to perfect the look, test products on your skin, confirm longevity, and discuss the timing and order for the morning itself.

For weddings and any occasion where the result needs to be exactly right, a trial is strongly recommended.

Bridal questions

Bridal FAQ.

Coverage, booking, deposits, trials, timing, and group bookings — answered.

What areas do you cover for bridal makeup?

Yana is based in London and travels to weddings across the city and surrounding areas. Travel fees apply outside central London and are calculated based on your location.

How do I book bridal makeup?

Send an enquiry with your wedding date, venue area, and number of people. Yana will confirm availability, provide pricing, and send deposit details to secure your date.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit is required to secure your wedding date. Details are confirmed at the time of booking.

Do you charge for travel?

Yes. A travel fee applies and is calculated based on your location. This is confirmed before you book — no surprises.

Is a bridal trial included?

The trial is a separate appointment and is priced accordingly. It is strongly recommended for all brides.

What if I have sensitive skin or allergies?

Please mention any skin concerns or allergies when you enquire. The trial is a good opportunity to test products and discuss alternatives if needed.

How long does bridal makeup take?

As a guide, bridal makeup takes approximately 1.5 hours. Bridesmaids and guests approximately 45 minutes each.

Can you accommodate a bridal party?

Yes. Please include the number of people when you enquire so Yana can plan timing and confirm pricing.
