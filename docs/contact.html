Booking questions.

A few essentials that help your enquiry move quickly.

How quickly will I hear back?

Yana aims to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours. For urgent questions, WhatsApp is the fastest option.

Do I need to pay a deposit to book?

Yes. A deposit is required to secure your date. Details are confirmed after your initial enquiry.

Is there a travel fee?

Yes, for locations outside central London. The exact fee is calculated based on your area and confirmed before booking.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with clean skin and no makeup if possible. Let Yana know in advance about any skin concerns, allergies, or sensitivities so the appointment can be planned accordingly.
