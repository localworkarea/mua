Booking questions

FAQ before you enquire.

The essentials about booking, travel, and how long your makeup will last.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry via the form or WhatsApp with your event date, location, and number of people. Yana will confirm availability and provide a quote. A deposit secures your date.

Do you travel to me?

Yes. Mobile service is available across London and surrounding areas. A travel fee applies and is calculated based on your area.

How long does makeup take?

Bridal makeup typically takes around 1.5 hours. Bridesmaids and guests approximately 45 minutes each.

How long does the makeup last?

With professional skin prep, long-wear products, and setting techniques, makeup typically lasts 8–14 hours depending on skin type, weather, and event conditions.
