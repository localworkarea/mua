Makeup trial

Why a trial makes a difference.

A bridal or pre-event trial is a dedicated appointment to perfect your chosen look, test products on your skin, check longevity, and discuss timing for the day itself. Highly recommended for weddings and any occasion where the result needs to be exactly right.

Good to know

Service & pricing FAQ.

The most common questions about what’s included and how pricing works.

What’s included in the price?

All makeup application is included. Lashes, travel, and trial sessions are priced separately unless stated otherwise.

How is the total price calculated?

The starting price covers the makeup service itself. Travel fees, early start charges, and any additions (such as bridesmaids or a trial) are added based on your specific booking details.

Are lashes included?

False lashes are available on request and may be priced separately. Please mention your preference when enquiring.

What about sensitive skin?

Please mention any allergies, sensitivities, or skin concerns when you enquire. A trial is particularly recommended if you have reactive skin — it allows time to test products and discuss alternatives.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry with your event date, location, number of people, and preferred style. Yana will confirm availability, provide pricing, and send deposit details to secure the date.
